## What Obsidian Club is

Obsidian Club is a closed, premium community platform for people connected
to BDSM culture (practicing, learning, or curious) — 18+ only.

It is **not a social network**. It is the operating system of a closed club,
where the actual product is status, belonging, trust, reputation, and
access.

**A user does not register. They are granted access.**

## The central figure

**Lord Obsidian** is the singular, irreplaceable image of the club's
founder and voice — not a member, not a level, a separate entity: a symbol,
an archetype, the center of gravity for the whole platform.

## Member levels (overview — full mechanics in [UX.md](UX.md))

1. Level I — Initiate
2. Level II — Member
3. Level III — Senior Member
4. Mentor
5. Master
6. Council Member

**Lord Obsidian sits outside this system.** Singular. Unreachable.

## Platform structure, at a glance

- **The Hall** (`/hall`) — a personal status dashboard, not a feed
- **Rooms** — general, thematic, level-gated, mentor/master/council-only,
  and city-based local circles
- **Content** — stories, articles, podcasts, lectures, manifestos, video,
  courses; a partly-free, partly-level-gated library
- **Events** — closed parties, dinners, meetups, trips; each gated by level,
  seat limit, dress code, and Trust Score
- **Marketplace** — merch, limited collections, tickets, digital
  collections; a vitrine, not a storefront

## Staged rollout

1. **Stage 1 — Landing Page** (this repo's `v0.1`): entry point, designed to
   create a sense of scarcity and selection.
2. **Stage 2 — Web Platform / PWA**: registration, profile, levels,
   reputation, rooms, events, basic content, the Hall, an admin panel.
3. **Stage 3 — iOS App**, after the web mechanics are proven.
4. **Stage 4 — Android App**.
5. **Stage 5 — Desktop**, if warranted.

In parallel with platform development: a 6-month audience warm-up across
Instagram, Threads, X, Facebook, YouTube Shorts, and YouTube (see
`CONTENT_SYSTEM.md`) — not this repo's concern, but the context for why the
Landing Page (`v0.1`) exists and is optimized to convert visitors into a
waitlist.

## Inspirations, explicitly not copied

Instagram (content presentation), Threads (fast thought-stream), Discord
(rooms), Reddit (topic discussion), LinkedIn (member status), Duolingo
(gamified progress), Rolex (premium, unattainable, aspirational belonging).
**Obsidian Club copies none of them — it is its own system.**
