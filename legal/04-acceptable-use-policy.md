## 1. Purpose and scope

Obsidian Club is a private, invitation-only community for **verified adults (18+)**
built on **consent, respect, and discretion**. This Acceptable Use Policy ("**AUP**")
sets the rules for everything you post and do on the Platform — posts, photos,
comments, chat rooms, and spaces. It applies to all members and all User Content.

The Platform permits sensual and erotic adult expression **within the bounds of
consent**. It is **not** a pornography service, an escort/dating service, or a venue
for commercial sexual services. Breaking these rules can lead to content removal,
suspension, or — for red-line violations — **immediate permanent termination without
appeal** (Section 6), and in serious cases, referral to authorities.

## 2. The foundation: the five laws and consent

Your participation is governed by the community's **Code of Conduct (the five
laws)**, the **Safety & Respect Guidelines**, and the **consent model** you accepted
at registration. In summary, and as binding rules:

1. **Consent is absolute.** Every interaction and every depiction of another person
   requires that person's specific, informed, freely given, and **revocable**
   consent. "No," silence, or withdrawal of consent must be respected immediately.
2. **Respect boundaries and red lines.** Honor stated limits. The red lines in
   Section 4 are never permitted, under any framing.
3. **Protect the circle.** What happens in the community stays in the community.
   Never expose, out, or redistribute another member's content or identity.
4. **Adults only, truthfully.** You are 18+, you represent yourself truthfully, and
   you never help anyone under 18 access the Platform.
5. **Safety over everything.** When in doubt, protect people. Report harm.

## 3. Content that is allowed (within consent)

Subject to the red lines below, members may share: personal writing and discussion;
artistic and sensual imagery; **nudity and erotic self-expression** of themselves or
of another consenting adult who has agreed to that specific posting on this Platform;
and consent-centered lifestyle content presented through the community's aesthetic.

## 4. Red lines — absolutely prohibited

The following are **never** allowed and are **red-line violations** subject to
immediate, non-appealable termination and, where applicable, legal reporting:

**4.1 Anything involving minors.** Any sexual, suggestive, or exploitative content
involving a person under 18 — real, purported, fictional, drawn, computer-generated,
or AI-generated — and any attempt to sexualize, groom, or contact a minor. This is a
zero-tolerance, reported-to-authorities category. [^minors]

**4.2 Non-consensual content and conduct.** Any content depicting or promoting
non-consensual sexual activity; any intimate image of a person shared without that
person's specific consent (including "revenge" content, hidden-camera/voyeur
content, and deepfakes/AI imagery of real people); coercion, blackmail, or pressure;
and ignoring or overriding withdrawn consent.

**4.3 Prohibited explicit content (the §2257 line).** Depictions of **actual
sexually explicit conduct** are **not permitted** on the Platform. For this Policy,
"actual sexually explicit conduct" means actual (not simulated) sexual intercourse,
oral/anal sex, masturbation, bestiality, sadistic/masochistic activity involving
actual sexual conduct, and **lascivious exhibition of the genitals or pubic area**,
as those terms are used in U.S. federal law (18 U.S.C. §2256). Sensual nudity and
erotic expression that does **not** meet this definition are allowed under Section 3.
[^explicitline]

**4.4 Doxxing and off-Platform leaking.** Publishing or threatening to publish
another member's real identity, location, contacts, or content outside the Platform;
screenshotting or downloading others' content for redistribution; or "outing" any
member.

**4.5 Commercial sexual services / trafficking.** Advertising, soliciting,
arranging, or facilitating prostitution, escorting, or any commercial sexual service;
and any human trafficking or sexual exploitation. This is prohibited regardless of
framing and is reported. [^fosta]

**4.6 Sale or transfer of access.** Selling, trading, sharing, or publishing
invitation tokens or account access; creating accounts to evade enforcement.

**4.7 Harassment, threats, hate, and violence.** Targeted harassment, stalking,
threats, incitement, or content promoting violence or hatred against people based on
protected characteristics.

**4.8 Other unlawful content.** Anything illegal under applicable U.S. law,
infringement of others' IP or privacy, malware, or attacks on the Platform.

## 5. General conduct rules

- **Get consent before posting anyone but yourself.** You must hold the depicted
  adult's specific, informed, revocable consent for the exact posting.
- **Keep it inside.** No capturing or redistributing community content or identities
  externally.
- **No minors, ever**, in content or as users. Report suspected minors immediately.
- **Report harm.** Use in-product reporting or safety@obsidianclub.online to flag violations,
  especially red lines.
- **No spam, scams, scraping, bots, or automated collection** without our written
  permission.
- **Respect moderation.** Do not evade bans, argue in bad faith with safety
  decisions, or retaliate against reporters.

## 6. Enforcement

6.1 **Range of actions.** We may, at our discretion, remove or label content, warn,
restrict features, suspend, or terminate accounts, with or without notice.

6.2 **Red-line violations.** For red-line violations (Section 4), we may
**immediately and permanently terminate** membership **without prior notice, cure
period, refund, or appeal**, preserve relevant content and logs, and report to
NCMEC and/or law enforcement where required or appropriate. Enforcement is a
community-safety measure at our discretion, not a legal adjudication. [^enforce]

6.3 **Cooperation and preservation.** We may preserve content, account data, and
logs relating to violations to enforce this Policy, protect members, and comply with
legal obligations, including CSAM reporting duties.

6.4 **No obligation to monitor.** We may, but are not required to, monitor content.
Our right to act does not create a duty to detect or remove any particular content.
[^230]

## 7. Reporting

To report a violation — especially anything involving a minor, non-consensual
content, or a safety threat — use in-product reporting or contact **safety@obsidianclub.online**.
Urgent real-world danger should also be reported to local emergency services.

## 8. Changes

We may update this Policy as the community and the law evolve. Material changes will
be notified and reflected in the "Last updated" date. Continued use means acceptance.

[^explicitline]: **Most important AUP call.** Because federal "sexually explicit conduct" includes *lascivious exhibition of the genitals or pubic area*, a policy that bans "actual sexually explicit conduct" while allowing "nudity" needs a crisp, workable line so moderators can apply it and so the Platform stays outside §2257 producer status. Counsel to finalize the exact boundary (e.g., permissible artistic nudity vs. prohibited lascivious genital display) and align it with the §2257 memo and moderation guidelines.
[^fosta]: This category is critical to the FOSTA-SESTA / Section 230 posture — the Platform must be unambiguously not a venue for commercial sexual services. Keep detection/removal and non-facilitation practices consistent with the Section 230 memo.
[^enforce]: See ToS §10 footnote — "no appeal" is enforceable as a term but should stay discretionary; ensure preservation/reporting steps satisfy §2258A and do not create a §2258A affirmative-monitoring problem.
[^230]: "No duty to monitor" supports Section 230(c) posture; confirm consistency with any statutory scanning/reporting duties and with actual moderation practice.
